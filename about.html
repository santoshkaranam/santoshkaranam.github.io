Building the Bridge Between Code & Science

I'm Santosh Karanam, currently working as a Guest Full Stack Developer in the Novel Data Ecosystems for Sustainability (NODES) Research Group at IIASA's Advancing Systems Analysis Program in Vienna, Austria.

I develop and support software applications to aid and enhance the institute's research. My work spans citizen science platforms, geospatial web applications, cloud-native architectures, and data processing pipelines. Before IIASA, I spent a decade in the industry at companies like Honeywell and DellEMC, building enterprise solutions.

I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication from Bangalore Institute of Technology. I'm also a C# Corner MVP (2022 Q2) and Member of the Month (April 2022), where I actively contribute technical articles on .NET, PowerShell, and GIS technologies.
